v1.4.18:
* Added optional tracker idle-state support for parked R2C clients. The
  tracker now advertises idle parking guidance in hello_ack, accepts a
  backwards-compatible idle message before clients close /ws/r2c, and
  shows parked instances as IDLE on /r2c.
* Idle requests are ignored while the client still owns an active drone
  lease, preserving owner/relay behavior during live flights.

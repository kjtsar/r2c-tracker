v1.4.17:
* Reduced idle coordination overhead on the tracker by throttling heartbeat-
  triggered zone_update broadcasts to once per minute by default, while
  preserving immediate delivery for owner changes, owner expiry, Drone
  Confirmation Save events, disconnects, and initial joins.
* Lowered noisy /ws/r2c lifecycle and heartbeat logging from routine idle/
  reconnect traffic, keeping abnormal or longer-lived closes visible for
  diagnostics.
* Added regression coverage to verify heartbeat acknowledgements are still
  sent every time even when idle zone-update fanout is throttled.
